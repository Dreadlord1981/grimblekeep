# Grimblekeep — accepted direction and open questions

This is the first small lore checkpoint, agreed with the user on 2026-11-15.
It records the approved Grubbin concept, not a complete world history.

## Grubbins

Grubbins are squat moss-green crypt custodians with broad shoulders, round bellies,
oversized ears, uneven tusks and heavy feet. They are grumpy, proud and slightly
grotesque, yet become endearing when watched going about their business. The
approved concept is `docs/references/grubbin-lair-concept.png`.

They traditionally tend old crypts. Humans keep restoring those crypts, installing
lighting and removing perfectly good bones. A keeper's dungeon offers somewhere
dark, secure and properly unpleasant. Grubbins consider themselves fearsome
guardians; their first priority is a decent bed. This is their self-image, not an
implemented combat ability or a final decision about their future profession.

Their Lair nests have lumpy mattresses, patched blankets and small personal
possessions. A stolen boot or a skull turned towards the wall "for privacy" gives
the humor a domestic, slightly macabre character. Creatures furnish personal bed
spaces as they move in; empty Lair tiles do not automatically contain beds.
They must physically visit and claim their chosen space before furnishing it;
reserving room capacity from a portal does not create a bed remotely.

The first species uses a one-tile bed. Future species can need larger rectangular
spaces, larger meals and different gold wages. Food and wage values are provisional
design requirements; M3A implements only beds, energy and rest.

## A dungeon community of specialists

The user wants the workforce character of Settlers alongside the dungeon, Heart,
portals and keeper-hand management of Dungeon Keeper. Some creatures dig and haul,
some cook, some craft doors or room machinery, and suitable creatures fight.
Not every creature is a combatant. Roles are capabilities and may overlap; new
species should not require a fixed civilian/soldier class hierarchy.

Basic Hatchery/Pig Pen food will be edible directly. A later Dining Hall can combine
cooking stations and tables, turning basic food into more satisfying meals and
improving mood. Exact yields, labor, wages and species talents remain to be tested.
No additional wood or stone inventory currencies are agreed.

## Still open

The keeper's identity, the Heart's deeper meaning, portal destinations, the wider
world and first specialist professions remain open. So do the second resident
species, final food-room theme and combat rules. Do not invent these as settled
canon or implement their mechanics merely because they are mentioned here.
